We want you to be satisfied with your purchase.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

If your item arrives damaged, faulty or incorrect, please contact us within 14 days of delivery.

Your right to cancel

If you are buying as a consumer, you have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving your goods, without giving a reason. You then have a further 14 days from telling us to send the goods back. This right applies in addition to, and separately from, your rights in respect of damaged, faulty or incorrect items.

Two exceptions apply:

  • Sealed goods that are not suitable for return for health protection or hygiene reasons cannot be returned once the seal has been broken.
  • This right to cancel does not apply to business, trade or wholesale orders.

Condition of returned goods

Returned products should be unused and in their original packaging unless they are faulty.

Refunds

Refunds will be processed to your original payment method once the returned goods have been inspected.

Before you return anything: get in touch with us first so we can confirm the details of your return and guide you through the next steps.
