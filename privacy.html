Information We Collect

When you use our website or place an order, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Billing and delivery address
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Payment details (processed securely through Stripe, PayPal or your card provider)
  • Order history
  • Website usage information through cookies

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Process payments
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Send marketing emails where you have given consent

Sharing Your Information

We only share your information with trusted third parties where necessary, including payment providers, delivery companies and service providers who help us operate our business.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Security

We use appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access or misuse.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to request access to your personal information, ask for corrections or request deletion where applicable.
